Join us

There is a place for you in this work.

CFTFIP exists to invite investors, stakeholders, and well-wishers to fund and shape a project too capital-intensive to build alone. Whatever your role, there is a way to contribute.

Ways to help

Choose the contribution that fits.

The project is strongest when knowledge, resources, and community accountability move together.

01

Fund the plan

Help cover the capital costs of land, buildings, equipment, water, inputs, community work, and implementation.

02

Partner with us

Bring an organization, network, or specialist perspective to one or more of the four programs.

03

Share expertise

Offer practical knowledge in agriculture, enterprise, environmental work, child protection, communications, or evaluation.

04

Advocate and listen

Help keep accountability and child protection visible, and share feedback that makes the work more useful to the community.

Feedback and questions

Good work should be open to correction.

If you have a question about the project, a suggestion for the plan, or a concern about how the work is being carried out, please get in touch. Feedback can be sent by email or shared by phone.

CFTFIP is a work in progress. Clear questions and constructive feedback help the project remain accountable to the community it is designed to serve.
