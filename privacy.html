Privacy

A simple approach to information.

This page explains what happens when you visit the CFTFIP site or choose to contact the project directly.

Information

What this site collects.

This website is a static informational site. It does not include a contact form, advertising trackers, or analytics tools. The pages are served as files, so visiting them does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information.

If you choose to email or call the project, you provide the information you decide to share, such as your name, contact details, organization, or question.

Use of information

How contact information is used.

Information sent by email or phone is used to respond to the question, discuss a partnership or funding conversation, share requested project information, or record feedback relevant to the project. CFTFIP does not sell personal information through this website.

If you contact the project, only share information that is appropriate for the conversation. You can ask what information is being held, request a correction, or ask for a conversation to be stopped by contacting the project using the details on the contact page.

External services

Fonts and hosting.

The site uses Google Fonts to load its typefaces and is hosted as a static website. Those services receive the ordinary technical request information needed to deliver fonts and page files. No additional analytics or advertising services are configured here.

This page is a plain-language starting point for the project's privacy practice. It should be reviewed alongside the project's operational and legal requirements before a formal launch.
