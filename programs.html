The work

Four programs, one shared future.

CFTFIP works on four fronts at once because food, income, environment, and child safety are one problem, not four.

The four plots

Practical work, connected by purpose.

Each program is designed to stand on its own while strengthening the conditions that make the others possible.

Plot I — Agriculture and food security

Growing more, and growing smarter.

Raise food and nutrition security for children and expectant mothers by improving crop and livestock productivity, extending access to appropriate technology, and strengthening pest and disease control.

What this includes

  • Pest and disease control in the field
  • Adoption of appropriate agricultural methods
  • Dietary intake, food access, diversity, and nutrition education
Plot II — Economic development

Livelihoods beyond the farm.

Strengthen small and medium enterprises in trade, agribusiness, communication technology, and the creative sector, including sports and entertainment, through entrepreneurship workshops that help young people build self-employment with lasting momentum.

What this includes

  • Enterprise skills for trade, agribusiness, communications, and creative work
  • Entrepreneurship workshops for young people
  • Support for small and medium enterprises and self-employment
Plot III — Environment and climate

Protecting the land everything depends on.

Embed resource efficiency, recycling, forest protection, drought-resilient crops, and climate-smart farming into daily practice so the environment can continue to support the community.

What this includes

  • Tree nursery production
  • Solar energy and biogas adoption
  • Drought-tolerant, improved local seed varieties
  • Double-digging and organic production methods
  • Soil erosion control and agro-biodiversity conservation
  • Rainwater harvesting and soil moisture management
Plot IV — Lobbying and advocacy

Accountability, and protecting children.

Push local and national representatives for accountable action while confronting child labour, sexual exploitation, child marriage, and female genital mutilation. The work is carried out with local government, law enforcement, and the community to keep children safe.

What this includes

  • Advocacy for accountable local and national leadership
  • Community-led protection from exploitation and abuse
  • Research to underpin the work throughout
Accountability

How we will know it is working.

A monitoring and evaluation plan built with program staff from day one keeps the work grounded in goals, indicators, evidence, and regular reporting.

MONTHLY

A running record of implementation progress.

QUARTERLY

Approved work plan compared with actual performance, with constraints and remedial actions identified.

SEMI-ANNUAL

A full account of implementation with clear recommendations for corrective action.

ANNUAL

Outputs, outcomes, the most significant changes of the year, and recommendations going forward.
